ECCLESIA — LANGUAGE DATA: SOURCES, CREDITS AND LICENCES
========================================================

This notice covers the analysed language data under /lingua/ (tokens, registry, occurrences, passages, index.json):
the headword, part of speech, parsing and gloss attached to each word of the Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek and Latin texts.
The rights of the texts themselves are recorded per edition in the library catalogue.

Some of this data comes from openly licensed datasets. They are NOT public domain. They are used with credit, as their
licences require, and the data derived from share-alike sources is offered under the same terms (see "Share-alike").
Each work's own credit and licence are also in /lingua/index.json and are shown in the Language Studio and the reader.

HEBREW AND ARAMAIC
------------------
Text: Westminster Leningrad Codex 4.20. Public domain.
Morphology and lemma numbers: Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible (OSHB), by the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible project,
  files dated up to 2019-01-13. CC BY 4.0. https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb
Headwords, glosses and roots: Brown, Driver and Briggs, A Hebrew and English Lexicon of the Old Testament (1906),
  public domain, in the Open Scriptures XML edition (CC BY 4.0); Open Scriptures Hebrew Lexical Index (CC BY 4.0);
  Strong's Hebrew Dictionary (1890), public domain, in the Open Scriptures JSON edition (CC BY-SA).
  https://github.com/openscriptures/HebrewLexicon   https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs
Changes made: the morphology codes were expanded into named features, prefixes and suffixes were separated from the
  stem, and each word was linked to one headword. Where the text has a written form and a read form (ketiv and
  qere), the read form is given. All 39 books are included.

GREEK NEW TESTAMENT
-------------------
Text: The Greek New Testament: SBL Edition (SBLGNT), edited by Michael W. Holmes. Copyright 2010 Society of Biblical
  Literature and Logos Bible Software. CC BY 4.0. https://sblgnt.com/   https://www.sblgnt.com/license/
Morphology and lemmas: MorphGNT, SBLGNT edition, version 6.12, by James K. Tauber and contributors. CC BY-SA 3.0.
  https://github.com/morphgnt/sblgnt
Glosses: Dodson Greek Lexicon, by John Jeffrey Dodson. Public domain.
Strong's numbers: Strong's Greek Dictionary (1890), public domain, in the Open Scriptures JSON edition (CC BY-SA).
Changes made: the editors' apparatus marks were removed from the text, the parsing codes were expanded into named
  features, and glosses were attached. All 27 books are included.

GREEK OUTSIDE THE NEW TESTAMENT (the Apostolic Fathers, Athanasius)
------------------------------------------------------------------
The analysis is automatic and has not been checked by a person; it may be wrong.
Inflection tables: Wiktionary, Ancient Greek entries, by Wiktionary contributors, as extracted by kaikki.org
  (Tatu Ylonen, Wiktextract). CC BY-SA 4.0. https://kaikki.org/   https://en.wiktionary.org/
Form index: MorphGNT (as above). CC BY-SA 3.0.
Glosses: Dodson Greek Lexicon. Public domain.
Changes made: the tables were turned into a form index and applied to the texts by the GoLearn Greek analyser.

LATIN
-----
The analysis is automatic and has not been checked by a Latinist; it may be wrong.
Dictionary and inflections: William Whitaker's Words. Free for any use, by its author's statement.
Headwords and glosses: Lewis and Short, A Latin Dictionary (1879), public domain, in the Perseus Digital Library's
  TEI edition. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://github.com/PerseusDL/lexica
Ranking of ambiguous forms: frequencies drawn from the Perseus Latin Dependency Treebank. CC BY-SA 3.0.
  https://github.com/PerseusDL/treebank_data  (no treebank text is included)
Changes made: the dictionaries were turned into a form index and applied to the texts by the GoLearn Latin analyser.
